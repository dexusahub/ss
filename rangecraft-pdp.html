Product Details & Specifications

The RangeCraft T1x MTR (Multi-Task Rimfire) is a Finnish-engineered precision platform built for shooters who refuse to compromise. Sharing its DNA with the legendary T3x centerfire line, the T1x MTR delivers the same ergonomics, trigger feel, and quality craftsmanship in a rimfire format — making it the ideal companion for small-game hunting and target practice alike.

Its cold hammer-forged, free-floating barrel is machined from premium Cr-Mo alloy, hand-crowned for muzzle consistency, and threaded to the receiver for repeatable zero retention. The adjustable single-stage trigger is crisp, clean, and tunable through the magazine well without tools.

  • Full-size ergonomics identical to T3x centerfire models — no relearning curve
  • Cold hammer-forged Cr-Mo barrel — free-floating for maximum accuracy
  • Two-position safety blocks both trigger and bolt handle simultaneously
  • Tool-free trigger pull adjustment from 2.2 to 4.4 lbs via magazine well
  • Picatinny rail integrated for seamless optics mounting
  • Synthetic stock with checkered grip — weatherproof and ambidextrous-friendly
