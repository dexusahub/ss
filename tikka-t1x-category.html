Engineering Excellence

What Sets the T1x Apart

Every feature of the T1x reflects Tikka's obsession with precision, reliability, and shooter confidence.

01
🔩
Cold Hammer-Forged Barrel

Highest-grade Cr-Mo alloy barrel produced with Finnish barrel technology. Hand-crowned muzzle and free-floating design delivers exceptional group consistency shot after shot.

02
⚙️
Smooth Bolt Action

Extremely rigid action with a 45° bolt opening angle, removable bolt handle, and one of the smoothest cycling actions on the rimfire market. Designed for accuracy and easy servicing.

03
🎚️
Adjustable Trigger System

Single-stage trigger with vertical grooves for grip even with gloves. Pull weight adjustable 1–2 kg through the magazine well. Optional single-set trigger available across all models.

04
🔒
Two-Position Safety

Blocks both the trigger and the bolt handle simultaneously. Clear visual and tactile indicators confirm safe and fire status — no ambiguity in the field.

05
📐
T3x Cross-Platform Ergonomics

Shares identical feel, stock geometry, and ergonomics with the Tikka T3x centerfire. Symmetrical design, checkered grip, and fine finish — ideal for cross-training between calibers.

06
📦
Fiberglass-Reinforced Magazine

Detachable box magazine with fiberglass-reinforced polymer construction. Holds 10 rounds (.22 LR) or 6 rounds (.17 HMR). Flush release prevents accidental drops.

07
🔇
Minimized Recoil & Noise

Rimfire calibers deliver comfortable shooting with significantly reduced noise and recoil versus centerfire — ideal for extended sessions, training, and small-game scenarios.

08
🔧
Tikka Accessory Ecosystem

Wide range of compatible stocks, optic mounts, and accessories through the Tikka accessory world. Modify and adapt the T1x to fit any shooting discipline or ergonomic preference.
